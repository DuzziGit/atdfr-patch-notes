Latest Update · October 29

October 29 update

A brand-new Cash Shop full of classic MapleStory clothes, your own MSW look on your characters, and a free pet to try at 2nd job. The earlier notes are still below.

New

The new Cash Shop

  • The 2009 Cash Shop, always open. Over 1,000 classic pieces from MapleStory's 2009 Cash Shop are always on sale, sorted into Hats, Overalls, Tops, Bottoms, Shoes, Gloves, Capes, Face, Eyes and Earrings. Gloves is a new section.
  • Today's Release. Every day the Today stall sells one premium release from later years of MapleStory (Royal Style labels and Premium Surprise Style Boxes). Every piece is sold on its own, never in a box. The stall changes at 00:00 UTC, and each release comes back when the stall comes round again.
  • Deal of the Day. The headline piece of today's release is 30% off, in both its boy and girl versions when it has them.
  • Pet of the Day. One pet a day that isn't otherwise on sale.
  • Transparent items for every slot, the weapon included. Hide a hat to show off your hair, or hide anything else you'd rather not see.
  • Underwear is in Bottoms.
  • Hair, faces and skin have left the shop, because your MSW look covers them now (see below). Anything you already own stays yours.
  • Who owns what: new clothes belong to the character who buys them. Pets are still shared by every character on your account.
  • Names and descriptions are in Korean and Chinese too.
New
☺

Your MSW look

  • Your characters can now wear your MapleStory Worlds avatar's hair, face and skin. Turn it on or off for each character in Options → MSW look.
  • Lock look keeps a character's look when you change your MSW avatar, so each character can look different: lock one, change your avatar, then lock the next. Update copies your current MSW look again.
  • New characters can start with it: pick My MSW look when you create one. Existing characters start with it on.

Update · October 24

October 24 update

A new chat, faces, party help and a punchier feel to combat.

New
…

New chat

The old chat box is gone. The new one sits out of the way at the bottom of the screen and shows your character name.

  • Open it with Enter, or click or tap the chat button. Enter sends and closes; Esc closes. Page Up makes it taller.
  • Tabs: All (everyone in the game, on every channel), Map, Party, Friends and Whispers.
  • Whisper anyone, not only friends. Click a name, type /w Name, or press R to reply to your last whisper.
  • Shortcuts: /a All, /m Map, /p Party, /f Friends.
  • Speech bubbles appear over players on your map, as in MapleStory.
  • Click a name to Whisper, Add friend, Invite to party, Inspect or Mute.
  • Options → Chat: choose what shows while chat is closed (new lines, only party/friends/whispers, or nothing), turn speech bubbles on or off, and manage your muted players.
New
☺

Faces

  • Press F1 to F7 to make a face: Hit, Smile, Troubled, Cry, Angry, Bewildered, Stunned. Everyone nearby sees it.
♣

Party

  • Haste from a Thief now speeds up party members on the same map.
  • One key, one door. Opening a door with a key opens it for every party member in the room, with an arrow pointing the way.
  • Story bosses: entering a boss door brings along partners standing at that door, as long as they've opened it themselves.
  • Move next to a partner's name takes you to them, anywhere you could walk to.
  • Faster respawns when several players hunt the same map, and a cleared map's monsters now come back together.
Changes
✦

Feel

  • Flash Jump reworked: it launches the moment you press it, with a smoother arc, and the burst comes from your heels.
  • Combat sounds and hits: louder bosses, hit flashes that match the hit, monsters that turn to face you, and a cleaner sound mix.
  • Boss bar fills in as the fight begins and drains away after the kill.
  • Area names paint in with a brush stroke.
  • Shorter trips when going back and forth between the Den, the Portal Room and places you've just visited.
  • Big hits are easier to read. Damage of 100,000 or more now shows short: 105K instead of 104,985, then 1.23M, 45.7B. Turn it off in Options → Short numbers to see every digit again.

Endgame

Echoes, rebuilt, and Echo Duels

Echoes now open at Lv 120, once you've finished the whole story. Until then the Echo list shows as a preview.

One endless ladder

1

Echo 1, 2, 3 and on, with no top. Each number uses one realm's floors (Red, Green, Yellow or Orange). It's the same for everyone and changes every Tuesday (UTC). Clearing in half par opens the next two numbers.

≈

The rain rises. Past par the water climbs on your run line, and at twice par the Echo floods and the run ends. Par is now set by the Echo number, and it's much shorter early on.

☂

Echo Umbrella (20,000 Meso at Winchester once you have access). When you'd die in a run you're asked "Open your umbrella?" for 8 s, with No as the default. Yes stands you up at 50% HP. You can use one per run. A Dry clear without one pays +25% Echo Dust and ranks higher.

★

Rewards. Your best clear each week leaves a cache of Echo Dust, shards and a gear piece. Your first clear each day pays +50% Dust. Bring friends: each extra party member adds +10% Dust and a chance at a second gear piece.

◆

Gear and amulet. Echo gear drops for Lv120, gets stronger with the Echo number and its grade, and is named for where it dropped ("Red Echo 8"). The amulet attunes itself when you have the shards. Each story boss pays one tier of shards once, and tiers now go past 20.

Also new: leaderboards at the level cap with a class filter (a new season starts now), a keep-going card (Next / Again / Portal Room), entering from anywhere safe, a Forfeit button, and an upgrade verdict on the result card. Affixes are renamed Thunderhead, Oilskin, Cloudburst, Squall, Overcast and Gust.

New PvP

Echo Duels

Race another player up a rising Echo, each in your own copy of the same floors. The water climbs behind you both, and if you flood or die you're out. The last one standing wins. You lose nothing but the duel.

Queue for a match (it follows you while you play) or challenge a friend, a party member or anyone nearby. Open the Duel tab with '. Players are matched by amulet tier, and Tinctures and umbrellas aren't allowed. All duels open at Lv120; ranked also needs the amulet.

30Pigment + a Forge Guard for a ranked win
15Pigment for a casual win
5Pigment for a finished loss

Your first 5 paid duels each day pay. A rematch with the same player pays once a day. Quitting or disconnecting pays nothing.

New systems

Realm sets and more

Every realm leaves something on you. Gear from a realm belongs to that realm's set: wear 3 pieces to wake its vice, and 5 to settle it into its virtue. Mix sets however you like, such as 3 of one and 3 of another. Until you're wearing 3 of a realm's set, every drop from that realm belongs to it; after that, only some do. Auto-equip still picks the stronger piece first.

Tougher bosses after Yellow

The White Monkey Chief, Ravana and the Holdfast have about twice the HP. They were falling in about 25 seconds; now they put up a real fight.

!

Late realms are dangerous again

Your armour now holds up less well against higher-level monsters, so from Blue onward they hit harder. Echo floors are unchanged.

⧗

Fuller rooms

More monsters everywhere, spread evenly across each room's platforms, and every room takes a few satisfying minutes to restore.

»

A faster start

The training ground's storm takes 50 kills, you reach Lv20 on the kill that clears it, and Tux sends you straight to Red. New characters start with stat and skill auto-assign on; untick it any time.

Orange

  • About a third more monsters.
  • Updrafts in every room, so you can reach every platform. The temple's stray platforms are gone.
  • Rooms glow amber from the start behind a grey haze that lifts as you fight, and the rain stops once a room is restored.

Blue

  • Every Blue room is relit as an aqua night, glowing from its own diver lamps.
  • You can grab its ropes and ladders straight from the floor.
  • In Blue, Indigo and Violet the lights are in colour from your first kill, and every monster's glow now matches its colour.
↑

Zakum's Last Stand

The story fight now shows a live arrow to the safe side of the wall.

Quality of life

Interface

Windows and controls

  • Skill icons pop when they come off cooldown, shake red when a cast is refused, and glow briefly when cast.
  • The Forge window is redesigned. The Hallmark column only shows on Echo gear.
  • "Sell downgrades", "Sell Etc items" and "Dismantle downgrades" each open one list with a tick per item.
  • Throw Away asks first and lets you choose how many. Toasts last longer (3.5 s).
  • The Stats window gets FORGE and LINKS tabs.
  • Set pieces show a thin line in their realm's colour in the bag and equipment.
  • Loot on the ground glows by grade, and a keycap over the nearest drop shows how to pick it up.
  • You can see other players' skill effects and pets (Options > Other players: Full / Light / Off), and you can take up to 3 pets out.

On phones

  • Combat Power shows on phones, with an ECHO row in the Den, the Portal Room and Echoes.
  • Tapping a bag item opens one compact card.
  • Edit HUD allows up to 10 skill buttons.
  • Flash Jump, Double Jump and Shadow Step jump for you on one tap.
Fixed

Bug fixes

  • Going to Character Select while dead no longer makes your next character load dead.
  • The Den and other maps no longer turn orange after Orange rooms or the Orange Echo.
  • Character Select's cave shows again after you return from the game.
  • The Husky pet no longer looks like it's crying.
  • Monsters knocked off a floor's edge no longer fall forever and block the room.
  • A boss's daily Legendary chest no longer gives gear above your level, and the Lv20 reward weapon now equips itself.
  • Phone: Flash Jump, Double Jump and mid-air Teleport use air charges like on PC, and you can read Edit HUD's "can't add that" messages.
  • The Wraith's Drowning Grasp now shows where it will land.
  • Iron Body no longer counts twice in Combat Power and Defense.
  • Selling in the shop is faster, the shop fits iPad screens, and phones and iPads run faster.
  • Ropes and ladders in Red, Green, Yellow and Orange (and their Echo floors) reach the floor, so pressing up at the foot grabs them.
  • Orange Echo ropes work. Echo floors have no padlocks, and Green's have fewer elites.
  • Korean and Chinese: tutorial arrows find translated keys, and phone players are told to tap instead of press.

Known issues

  • Channels are new. Tell us if switching misbehaves.
  • If you switch the language from Korean to Chinese in game, the title art stays Korean until you restart.
